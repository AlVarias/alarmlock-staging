Our Story & Standards

25 Years Keeping Auckland Properties Secure

AlarmLock has been operating in the Auckland security industry for over 25 years. Whether you are locked out, upgrading to a Yale digital lock, rekeying a new home, or setting up a commercial master key system, we deliver reliable workmanship at a fair, transparent price.

From our East Tamaki workshop at Unit 3, 19 Ormiston Road and mobile service vehicles covering Papakura and Auckland-wide, we combine one of the broadest ranges of key blanks in the region with the latest precision key-cutting machinery.
